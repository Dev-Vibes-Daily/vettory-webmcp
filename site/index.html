Tools with an unresolved, material risk are held off the catalog on a private review bench until a person can verify them. Published tools may still carry disclosed watch-outs — that's transparency, not a gap; the open questions are named right on the card. We also don't rate whole categories of unknown vendors — smaller no-code SMS services (Fleece AI, Whippy, GReminders) and several early agent-payment startups sit there now, unlisted on purpose.
